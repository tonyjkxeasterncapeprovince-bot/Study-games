What works here?

YouTube: Supported videos can play directly in the player. Some owners disable embedding.
TikTok: Individual public videos may play. The whole TikTok feed isn't embedded.
Instagram: Some public posts/reels may display. Sign-in or privacy settings may prevent playback.
Roblox: Use the Roblox shortcut to open its games normally; the Roblox client can't run inside a web player.
Facebook, Snapchat, and more: Search or use quick links. Their complete sites generally cannot be displayed inside an iframe.
School settings: A permitted embed may still be blocked by device or network rules. This site does not hide browsing or change monitoring.
